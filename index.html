The problem

Your workshop tool went away, and the replacement is worse.

Butter shut down. The parts people actually used got folded into something bigger and more general, and the thing that made a session feel run rather than attended did not survive the move.

So facilitators went back to a video call with a slide deck and a phone timer. It works. It just puts all the structure back in your head, where it was before, and everyone can tell.

What it is

A video platform built around the agenda, not the grid.

Margarine is a workshop tool for people who run sessions for a living. You build the agenda first and the session runs itself against it, so you spend the hour facilitating instead of stage managing.

  • Agenda and timeboxing — the plan is visible to everyone and the clock moves on its own.
  • Breakouts — split the room and pull it back without losing anyone.
  • Polls, reactions, hand raise and a speaker queue — the quiet half of the room gets a way in.
  • Recording and AI recaps — the session writes up its own notes.
  • Templates — start from a format that already works instead of a blank agenda.
  • Sounds and music — the small things that made a session feel like a room.
How it works

Three steps, same as the session you already run.

  1. Build the agenda. Blocks, timings, what happens in each one. Start from a template or your own.
  2. Send one link. People arrive in a waiting room that does not feel like a parking lot.
  3. Run it. The timer drives, breakouts open and close, and the recap lands when you finish.
Who it is for

Worth being clear about this.

For you if

  • You facilitate workshops, trainings or cohorts and the structure matters
  • You used Butter and have not found anything that fits since
  • You run the same formats repeatedly and want them saved
  • You are a team of one to ten, not a procurement committee

Not for you if

  • You need a general meeting tool for every call your company makes
  • You want webinars to a thousand people
  • You need SSO, SOC 2 and a security review before you can try something
  • Your sessions are unstructured and that is on purpose
